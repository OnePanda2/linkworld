Privacy policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

Link World (linkworld.siddheshthapa.com) helps you make a link-in-bio landing page with ChatGPT. It's run by Siddhesh Thapa. This page says, in plain words, what we collect, why, and what we never do.

The short version

  • Your screenshots and your ChatGPT conversation never reach us.
  • A page preview lives inside its own link. We don't store it unless you ask to own the page.
  • If you own a page, we keep your chosen address, your email and the page itself.
  • Visitor counts on owned pages use no cookies and store no IP addresses.
  • We don't sell data, show ads, or use tracking cookies.

When you make a page in ChatGPT

The Link World plugin is a set of instructions that runs inside ChatGPT. Your screenshots, your answers and the conversation are handled by OpenAI under OpenAI's privacy policy. We don't receive them.

ChatGPT turns your page into a link. Everything on the page (your name, words, colours and links) is packed into the part of the link after the "#". Browsers don't send that part to any server, so opening your link shows your page without us storing it.

When you ask to own a page

We keep:

  • the address you choose (for example linkworld.siddheshthapa.com/yourname);
  • your email address, to tell you when your page is live and, later, about your plan;
  • the page itself, so we can put it live.

We also keep a scrambled code made from the day and your connection, only to limit how many requests one sender can make in a day. It can't be turned back into your IP address, and it changes every day.

When someone visits an owned page

To show you how your page is doing, we count:

  • views and which links were tapped;
  • where visitors came from (for example Instagram or Google);
  • their country (from the network, not from location services);
  • whether they used a phone, tablet or computer.

We don't use cookies and we don't store IP addresses. Each visitor is counted once a day through a scrambled code that changes every day and can't be turned back. Preview pages are never counted.

Services we rely on

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) hosts the website. Like any web host, it may log visitors' IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces on pages. Your browser fetches them from Google, which receives your IP address to do so. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare runs the service that counts visits and takes requests to own pages, once it's switched on. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Requests to own a page that never go live are deleted after 90 days. Visit counts are kept while your page is live and deleted within 30 days after it closes. You can ask us to delete everything sooner.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete what we hold about you, or close your page, at any time. Write to us (below). We answer within 30 days. If you're in the EU, UK, Canada or Australia, you also have the rights your local privacy law gives you, including complaining to your data protection authority.

Age

You must be 16 or older to own a Link World page.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If you own a page and the change matters, we'll email you.

Contact

Open a support request on GitHub: github.com/OnePanda2/linkworld/issues.

Link World isn't made by, or affiliated with, OpenAI.
