How it works

About two minutes, start to finish.

  1. Have your screenshots ready

    Your Instagram profile and two or three posts you love. If there's a website or a look you'd like yours to feel like, screenshot that too.

  2. Tap through a few questions

    In ChatGPT: what should visitors do first, and who are they? Every answer is one tap, or type your own. ChatGPT reads the rest from your screenshots.

  3. Open your page

    It fits phones, tablets and computers. Not quite right? Say what to change, or ask for a completely different one.

Questions

Good to know.

Do you see my screenshots?

No. They stay in your ChatGPT conversation. When you open your page, our site only receives what's on the page: your name, words, colours and links.

Is it really free?

Making pages is free, and you can make as many as you like. Owning one is free for 14 days. After that, a small monthly price keeps it live. Prices are announced when owning opens.

Can I change my page later?

Yes. Ask in the same ChatGPT chat ("swap the green for black", "add my newsletter") and you'll get the updated page.

Who can use Link World?

Anyone 16 or older with links to share: creators, artists, coaches, small shops, musicians.

Who makes Link World?

Siddhesh Thapa, an independent maker. Link World is part of siddheshthapa.com.
