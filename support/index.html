How can we help?

Most answers are below. If yours isn't, write to us.

My page link doesn't open

The link is long because your whole page is inside it. If even a little is cut off, the page can't open. Copy the link again from ChatGPT, all of it. Or ask ChatGPT for your "page code" and paste it at linkworld.siddheshthapa.com/open.

I want to change something on my page

Go back to the same ChatGPT chat and say what to change: "use my logo colours", "add my podcast", "make it calmer". You'll get a new link. Want something completely different? Ask for another design.

ChatGPT didn't read my screenshots well

Use clear, full-screen screenshots: your profile (with your name, bio and link visible) and two or three posts. If something's still wrong, just tell ChatGPT the right details.

I want to own my page

Open your page and tap Own this page at the bottom. Owning is free for 14 days.

Someone made a page pretending to be me

Write to us with the page's link. We take down impersonation quickly.

Delete my data

Write to us from the email you used, and we'll delete your page and everything we hold about you within 30 days. Previews you never owned aren't stored by us at all.

Contact

Open a support request on GitHub: github.com/OnePanda2/linkworld/issues.
